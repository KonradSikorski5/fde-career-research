## Details

### I1
**Palantir — Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Internship – Commercial** · Chicago, IL · [Original posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/d5486403-c050-4920-b2e0-91b69b61ebb2)

- **Work:** embed with a customer; build custom apps, LLM workflows, and production solutions; own stakeholder relationships "from the users in the weeds to the executives."
- **Requires:** engineering degree (CS, ECE, math, etc.); one or more languages (Python, Java, C++, TypeScript/JavaScript); graduating Dec 2027 or Spring 2028.
- **Notes:** 25–50% travel expected.

### I2
**Palantir — Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Internship – US Government** · Washington, DC · [Original posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/e6ff8bf2-135e-474d-ad37-24f490ae1dd2)

- **Work:** small teams, minimal supervision; architecture, large-scale data, custom web apps, talking with customer executives.
- **Requires:** willingness to undergo a US government background investigation; one or more languages; graduating 2028; should be your final internship.
- **Notes:** 25–50% travel preferred.

### I3
**Palantir — Year at Palantir, FDSE Internship – Commercial** · New York, NY · [Original posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/e6789b17-62fb-4226-a079-f8c17ff19e2d)

- **Work:** a year-long internship building software and helping customers deploy AI solutions.
- **Requires:** pursuing an engineering-type degree; one or more languages; **hands-on experience applying AI** (coursework or personal projects count).
- **Notes:** open to freshmen, sophomores, juniors, and seniors. Clearance eligibility is "a plus" for commercial roles.

### I4
**Palantir — Forward Deployed Infrastructure Engineer, Internship – US Government** · Washington, DC · [Original posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/3db7e40a-28e0-4ad1-96c5-93de5bc96aa9)

- **Work:** deploy and operate software in production; monitoring, alerting, configuration management, upgrades; automate manual runbooks.
- **Requires:** active US security clearance or eligibility to get one before starting; strong proficiency in a language (Python, Java, Go, C++, TS/JS); graduating 2028.
- **Notes:** the infrastructure/DevOps flavor of FDE.

### I5
**Netic — Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), Intern, 2026–2027** · San Francisco, CA · [Original posting](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/netic/ffd6af1a-cebb-41f5-ac1d-e695adfd067c)

- **Work:** build multi-modal AI agents (text and voice) for service businesses; map customer workflows; A/B test models.
- **Asks for:** production systems in Python (asyncio, FastAPI) and TypeScript (Node, React); LLM, TTS and speech APIs, RAG, vector databases, evaluation frameworks.
- **Notes:** 12-week internship. The job board lists compensation as $138K–$160K without saying how that converts for a 12-week term.

### I6
**Composio — Forward Deployed Engineering Internship (Fall 2026 & Winter 2027)** · San Francisco, CA · [Original posting](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/composio/6b742321-a1e6-46d7-88d1-103bae44df23)

- **Work:** customers run Composio in their own environments. You'd work on Helm charts, upgrades and rollbacks, and onboarding, and debug Kubernetes, Temporal workflows, and auth.
- **Asks for:** building in environments you don't control (homelab, VPC, self-hosted); Linux; Kubernetes/Helm curiosity; having built with LLMs; writing a clear root-cause analysis (RCA).
- **Notes:** the posting says that for a very strong candidate, "nothing is a must per-se." Bonus items: Postgres, Temporal, and open-source or hackathon wins. The Fall 2026 term has already started, but the posting was still listed.
