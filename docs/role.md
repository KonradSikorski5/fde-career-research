# What the role does

A summary of what the 19 postings describe. Each point cites the postings it comes from. Open [Internships](/internships) or [Full-time](/full-time) to see the details.

## One-sentence definition

An FDE is an engineer **placed with a customer** who owns a problem from the first conversation to working production software, and then reports what they learned back to the product team. [I1](/internships#i1), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F9](/full-time#f9)

## The work, step by step

| Step | What postings say you do | Cited in |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | Learn the customer's workflow; turn a vague business problem into a defined one | [F5](/full-time#f5), [F6](/full-time#f6), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F9](/full-time#f9), [I5](/internships#i5) |
| 2. Scope | Set success criteria, sequence delivery, make scope/speed/quality trade-offs | [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7) |
| 3. Build | Custom apps, LLM workflows, agents, integrations with customer systems | [I1](/internships#i1), [I5](/internships#i5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F9](/full-time#f9), [F10](/full-time#f10) |
| 4. Deploy & run | Ship to production; monitoring, upgrades, rollback, debugging | [I4](/internships#i4), [I6](/internships#i6), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F8](/full-time#f8) |
| 5. Measure | Evaluations, A/B tests, adoption and workflow impact | [I5](/internships#i5), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F9](/full-time#f9) |
| 6. Feed back | Bring field patterns back to product and research teams | [I5](/internships#i5), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F8](/full-time#f8), [F13](/full-time#f13) |

## Four flavors seen in these postings

- **Application / AI FDE.** Builds LLM apps and agents for the customer. This is the most common flavor here. [I5](/internships#i5), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F9](/full-time#f9), [F10](/full-time#f10), [F12](/full-time#f12)
- **Data/platform FDE.** Builds on the employer's own platform with large-scale data. [I1](/internships#i1)–[I3](/internships#i3), [F1](/full-time#f1), [F4](/full-time#f4), [F8](/full-time#f8)
- **Infrastructure FDE.** Deploys and operates the product in customer environments: Kubernetes, Helm, monitoring, automation. [I4](/internships#i4), [I6](/internships#i6), [F3](/full-time#f3)
- **Domain FDE.** A domain expert (e.g., finance) who builds AI workflows; the coding bar is lower. [F13](/full-time#f13)

## What it's like

- **Customer contact is constant**, often up to executives. [I1](/internships#i1), [F6](/full-time#f6), [F11](/full-time#f11)
- **Travel** is stated in 7 of 19 postings, ranging from up to 25% to 30–50%. [I1](/internships#i1), [I2](/internships#i2), [F1](/full-time#f1), [F4](/full-time#f4), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F11](/full-time#f11)
- **Ownership from day one**, including for interns. Palantir says interns are "not … handed a ticket queue." [I1](/internships#i1), [I2](/internships#i2)
- **Government work** may need a background check or security clearance. [I2](/internships#i2), [I4](/internships#i4)

## How this differs from nearby roles

This comparison is **my interpretation**, not taken from the postings:

- vs. **Software engineer:** same coding, but you are judged on a customer's outcome, not a product team's backlog.
- vs. **Solutions/sales engineer:** FDEs build and own production systems, not just demos. Kepler lists "Solutions Engineer" as acceptable prior experience. [F11](/full-time#f11)
- vs. **Consultant:** FDEs write the code themselves. [F5](/full-time#f5) says "contribute directly in the code."
