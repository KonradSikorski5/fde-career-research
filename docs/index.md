# Forward Deployed Engineer: career research

A working collection of **real FDE job postings**, organized so I can compare them. Every posting was checked on its employer's own job board on **2026-11-12**.

## The role in three lines

- FDEs are software engineers who **work directly with a customer**, learn the customer's problem, and build and ship the fix inside the customer's systems. [I1](/internships#i1), [F5](/full-time#f5)
- Most current postings center on **deploying AI**: LLM apps, agents, retrieval (RAG), and evaluations. [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F9](/full-time#f9)
- Expect **travel** (often 20–50%) and a lot of talking with non-engineers. [I1](/internships#i1), [F5](/full-time#f5), [F7](/full-time#f7), [F11](/full-time#f11)

## How to read this site

- **IDs**: `I1–I6` are internships, `F1–F13` are full-time. Every finding cites the IDs it came from.
- **Status**: "Open" means the posting was live on the employer's board on 2026-11-12. Expired or removed postings are listed separately on [Sources](/sources#unavailable-or-not-verified).
- **Pay** is shown only when the posting states it. Nothing is estimated.
- **Not a full market survey.** This covers 19 postings from 11 employers. They were picked to show the range from internships to senior roles.
